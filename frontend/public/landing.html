✨ FEATURES

Everything you need for on-chain attendance

From gasless check-ins to blockchain-verified certificates — built for attendees, organizers, and collectors.

⚡

Gasless Check-In

Sign with your wallet using EIP-712. No ETH required — the relayer pays all on-chain gas costs on your behalf.

🏅

POAP Badges

Earn a soulbound ERC-1155 NFT badge for every event attended. Non-transferable proof that you were there.

🪙

VRYF Token Rewards

Earn ERC-20 VRYF tokens for check-ins, badge claims, and milestone achievements — automatically.

🏆

Milestone NFTs

Unlock Bronze, Silver, and Gold tier NFTs at 5, 10, and 20 events attended.

📜

Blockchain Certificates

Tamper-proof attendance certificates issued on-chain with a unique QR code for instant public verification.

📊

Leaderboard

Ranked attendance board with ENS name resolution — your .eth name instead of a raw wallet address.

🛠️

Organizer Dashboard

Deploy events, generate join codes, confirm attendees, and issue certificates from one dashboard.

🔐

Tamper-Proof Records

Every check-in lives on the blockchain. No central authority can alter or delete your attendance history.

⚙️ HOW IT WORKS

From wallet to certificate in minutes

A simple, gasless flow from check-in to on-chain reward.

1

Connect Your Wallet

Use MetaMask or WalletConnect to sign in. No account or email required.

2

Select an Event

Browse available events and select the one you're attending.

3

Enter the 6-Digit Join Code

Get the code from the organizer and enter it to initiate check-in.

4

Sign the EIP-712 Message

Sign a gasless message in your wallet. The relayer submits the transaction on-chain — you pay nothing.

ZERO GAS COST
5

Organizer Confirms Attendance

The organizer validates your attendance, recording it permanently on-chain.

6

Claim POAP Badge & VRYF Tokens

Claim your soulbound NFT badge and earn VRYF tokens. Milestone NFTs unlock at 5, 10, and 20 events.

7

Receive Your Certificate

The organizer issues a tamper-proof attendance certificate on-chain with a scannable QR code.
